# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please do not disclose security vulnerabilities publicly.

To report a vulnerability related to the Soup website or Soup CLI, please email:
**security@trysoup.dev** or contact the team at **team@trysoup.dev**.

Include:
- A clear description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- Steps to reproduce or proof of concept.
- Affected components or dependencies.

We will acknowledge reports promptly and coordinate a fix prior to any disclosure.
